Checks of the fast-clearing approximation and its predicted adjustment dynamics. All simulations use log utility, 100 non-whale agents, a fixed fundamental eta = 0.5, and (where present) a whale with fixed valuation W = 0.7. A-C use initial whale budget share rho_0 = 0.3, stubbornness s = 0.3, exact private signals, and h = 0, 0.1, ..., 0.9. States are recorded after trading and before belief updates, over 100 independent runs per h; A shows periods 20-99, while B-C show all periods from 0 to 99. (A) Observed CLOB reference price against the competitive clearing price calculated from contemporaneous portfolios and valuations; black dashed line: equality of the observed and theoretical prices (m_t = p_t*). (B) Median absolute reservation-price gap across agents, then median across runs, measured in ticks (tick = 0.01). Bands show the median across runs of the agent-level 10th and 90th percentiles, for h = 0, 0.5 and 0.9 only. The dotted line marks one tick. (C) Change in the whale's mark-to-market wealth share from its initial 30% share, in percentage points (pp); lines show medians across runs, with zero marking the initial share. Shading shows the 10th-90th percentiles across the 100 independent runs for each herding value; these bands describe variation across runs, not confidence intervals for the median. (D) Recovery from a common initial belief shock of -0.15 without a whale, at s = 0.3. Solid curves: median across 100 independent runs; bands: 10th-90th percentiles across runs; dashed curves: the predicted geometric decay, scaled to the simulated first post-shock deviation. The dotted line marks one tick. (E) Recovery time after a common -0.15 belief shock, for rho = 0.3 and s = 0.2 or 0.9. For each run, the settled price is estimated by the median of the final 20 periods. Settlement requires the tail's 10th-90th percentile spread to be no greater than two ticks and its two half-window medians to differ by no more than one tick. Recovery is the first of five consecutive periods within one tick of that settled price, searched before the final estimation window. Time zero is the first realised post-shock price; the externally imposed initial price is excluded. Each condition has 100 independent runs. Symbols show median recovery times among settled runs (97-100 per condition), with 10th-90th percentile intervals. Lines show the continuous analytical recovery time T_epsilon = max{0, ln(epsilon/|p0*-pS|)/ln(kappa)}, evaluated on a dense herding grid without integer rounding, with epsilon = 0.01 and kappa = (1-h)s + h(1-rho). Here p0* = (1-rho)(eta-0.15) + rho W is the ideal clearing price for the initial shocked valuations, and pS = eta + deltaS is the theoretical equilibrium from the constant-wealth-share expression for deltaS. These benchmarks use only model parameters; the empirical recovery times use each run's estimated settled price. The comparison therefore includes departures from ideal initial clearing as well as differences in subsequent adjustment speed. Unsettled runs are flagged in the diagnostic report; unrecovered settled runs are right-censored and retained when calculating quantiles. An x denotes a condition with an unidentifiable recovery-time interval. The geometric benchmark remains approximate in the finite CLOB. In-panel equations state the approximations being checked, not identities assumed for the simulated order book. In A, w denotes mark-to-market wealth (all agents have log utility); in D, delta_t = m_t - eta.

Editorial note: this caption describes the five-panel assumptions figure. The supplied manuscript's Figure 5 caption describes six different panels and must be replaced if this figure is used there. Panels A-C here use equal initial non-whale budgets of 500, rather than the heterogeneous budgets described in manuscript Section 8.5. Panel E uses fresh simulated paths, stored in .simulation_cache for reuse. A diagnostic fit excludes the imposed initial price, estimates a run-specific settled price, and uses the initial transient above one tick. Some paths overshoot or settle too quickly for a reliable single convergence factor, so E reports recovery times. recovery_diagnostics.json records per-run fit quality, fitted kappa, settlement and censoring flags, and condition-level sample counts. All panels use 100 independent runs per setting.
